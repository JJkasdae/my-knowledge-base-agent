# Note-Taking in Lectures

For two years I typed out what was on the slides. I had beautiful notes and I could not answer a single question about any of them a week later.

The slides already exist. Copying them is transcription, and transcription feels like work while asking almost nothing of you.

What I do now is write down two things. The thing the lecturer said that was not on the slide, and any moment where I noticed I had lost the thread. The second one is more useful and I nearly always want to skip it, because writing "I do not understand this" feels like admitting something in a room full of people who look fine.

Fewer notes, much uglier, and I can actually use them. The ones I flagged as lost get picked up at the end of the week.
