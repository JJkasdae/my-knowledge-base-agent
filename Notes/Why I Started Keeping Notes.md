# Why I Started Keeping Notes

Because I kept having the same realisation twice.

Something would land, usually in a tutorial or on the walk home, and it would feel obvious and permanent. Three weeks later I would arrive at the same thing from scratch and it would feel just as new.

I do not think the problem is memory. I think the problem is that none of it was ever written anywhere I would walk past again.

So: one idea per note, in my own words, and links between them so that finding one drags the others along with it. It is early days. Ask me in a year whether it worked.
