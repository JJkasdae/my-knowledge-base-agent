# Applying for Internships

Said from the start that I wanted a research internship. Something with a supervisor, something that would read as real work rather than admin.

Went back through my sent folder tonight and counted. Eleven applications sent. Ten of them are marketing or general graduate programs. One is research.

The ten were not a decision. They were the ones with a form that took fifteen minutes instead of a cover letter and two referees. I did the easy ones and told myself I was building momentum.

The two research ones I actually want are still sitting in drafts. One of them closed on Friday.
