# Friday Reset

A short pass at the end of the week. Go back through the week's notes, mark off whatever is done, and carry exactly one unfinished thing into Monday.

Started doing it because the week would end and I would have no idea what had actually moved. Writing things down does not help if nothing brings you back to them.

Takes about twenty minutes if I do it in the afternoon. If I leave it until after dinner it does not happen at all.
