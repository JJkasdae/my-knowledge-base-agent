# Cognitive Load

There is a limit to how much I can hold in my head at once, and I keep finding it the hard way.

Four assignments, a shift roster, and two group chats I am supposed to be organising. None of them is hard on its own. Holding all of them at the same time is what is hard, and it is why I can sit down for two hours and produce nothing.

Writing something down is not really about remembering it later. It is about being allowed to stop holding it now.

Which is also why the notes have to be somewhere I trust. If I am not certain a thing is written down, I keep holding it anyway, and then I have paid the cost without getting the benefit.
