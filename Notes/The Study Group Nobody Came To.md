# The Study Group Nobody Came To

Set this up in March. Asked around after the tutorial, asked again in the group chat. Nine people said yes and a few of them were properly enthusiastic about it.

Booked a room for six weeks. Two people came to the first one. One came to the second. Nobody came to the third and I cancelled the rest.

What I keep going back to is that nobody lied to me. They all wanted a study group. Saying yes in a group chat costs nothing, and turning up at four on a Wednesday costs an afternoon.

Next time I would make agreeing cost something before I book anything. Ask people to pick the time themselves, or to bring one question each.

Cancelling it is, embarrassingly, the only thing I wrote down in an end-of-week pass all semester.
