# The Thing I Keep Not Starting

The big piece of work this semester. I have had it since week two and I have opened the document maybe six times.

What I do instead is smaller things that feel like progress. Reorganising the folder. Finding three more sources I will not read. Rewriting the same opening paragraph so it sounds more confident.

If anyone asked me what my priority is this semester I would say this project without hesitating, and I would mean it.

The file history says something else. Last real edit was eleven days ago, and the six edits before that all happened in one afternoon, which was the afternoon before a meeting about it.

Starting seems to need a deadline standing over me. I have not worked out how to fake one I actually believe in.
