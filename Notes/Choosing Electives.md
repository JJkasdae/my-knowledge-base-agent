# Choosing Electives

Spent a whole evening on this. Had a proper list of criteria: how much it would actually teach me, whether it connects to what I want to do after, whether it is assessed by essays or exams.

Picked the one my two friends picked. It meets none of the criteria. It has a Tuesday afternoon slot and people I know in the room.

I am not saying that was wrong. It might turn out to be the best call of the semester. But I should stop pretending the evening with the list did any of the deciding.

Worth remembering next time. If I am going to choose on timetable and company, I can do that in five minutes and spend the evening on something else.
