# Weekly Review

Set this up in February. Every Friday afternoon: read back what I wrote that week, close off anything finished, pull one thing forward into next week.

The point is not tidiness. It is that a decision I made on Monday is invisible by Thursday unless something makes me look at it again.

Twenty minutes when it works. It works when I do it before the day is over instead of saving it for the evening.

Worth being honest about the record, though. I would call this a weekly habit and I would say so if anyone asked. Going back through the dates, I have done it four times in five months. What I believe about my own routine and what the dates show are not the same thing.
