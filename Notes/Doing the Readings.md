# Doing the Readings

Reading something start to finish is the wrong shape for the job. The order it is written in serves the person who wrote it. By page four I have carefully absorbed the method of a study I have not yet decided is relevant to me.

Most weeks there are five of these and time for two. The skill is not reading faster, it is deciding sooner.

## The three-pass read

What works instead is going over the same piece three times, each time for a different question.

First pass: title, abstract, headings, figures, last paragraph. Is this worth my time at all. Two minutes, and most things stop here.

Second pass: read it properly, skip the fine detail and the numbers. What is it claiming, and does the argument hold together.

Third pass, only for the few that survive: work through the parts I would have to explain to somebody else. This is the only pass where I write anything down.

The failure mode is skipping the first pass because the thing is already open in front of me. Then I have spent an hour on something the abstract would have ruled out in two minutes.
